Questions

Before you start

How is this different from a template?

A template gives everyone the same headings to fill in. Bearing writes the content: specific channels, a dollar split of your actual budget, and weekly tasks that fit the time you have.

How long does the questionnaire take?

Most people finish in about eight minutes. Only a few questions are required, but the more detail you give, the more specific the plan.

What if my budget is very small?

Choose "Under $500". The plan will lean on free and low-cost channels such as Google Business, partnerships and email, and tell you what to skip.

Can I change my answers later?

Your answers stay in this browser until you pay, so you can go back and adjust them. After purchase you can generate up to two new versions of your plan at no extra cost.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled securely by Stripe. You can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your plan starts writing as soon as the payment goes through, and you'll get a receipt by email.

Is the plan written by a person?

No. It is written by an AI strategist from your answers. Treat it as a strong first draft and check it against what you know about your market.

Where can I find my plan later?

Bookmark the page your plan appears on. That link always shows your latest version.
